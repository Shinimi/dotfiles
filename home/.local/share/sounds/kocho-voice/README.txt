胡蝶しのぶ voice lines – put your own clips here (ogg, oga, mp3, wav, flac). Made by Claude.

Name each file after the moment it should play:
  desktop-login        when you log in            (e.g. a greeting line)
  desktop-logout       when you log out
  message-new-instant  new notification
  dialog-warning       important notification
  dialog-error         something failed
  complete             a long task finished
  screen-capture       screenshot taken
  device-added         device plugged in

Example:  desktop-login.ogg   message-new-instant.mp3

Several clips for one moment? Use desktop-login-1.ogg, desktop-login-2.ogg … and one is picked at random.
If there's no clip for a moment, the koto sound plays instead.
